# The Last Signal
# The Last Signal — Unity Project

Welcome to the repository of **The Last Signal**, a 2D Atmospheric Sci-Fi Exploration game built with Unity.

## 1. Introduction & Team
- **Game Title:** The Last Signal
- **Genre:** 2D Adventure / Sci-Fi Exploration / Mystery
- **Art Style:** Stylized pixel art with atmospheric lighting, deep space backgrounds, and retro-futuristic environments inspired by *Lone Survivor* and classic sci-fi games.

## 2. Core Idea & Lore
The player takes on the role of a lone astronaut stationed aboard an abandoned research outpost orbiting a distant planet. After receiving a mysterious transmission from an unknown source, the astronaut begins investigating the station's deserted corridors, damaged equipment, and forgotten research facilities.

As the player explores the outpost, they discover strange signals, uncover fragments of the station's history, and slowly realize that the transmission may be connected to the disappearance of the original crew.

The game focuses on atmospheric exploration, environmental storytelling, and puzzle-solving, creating a sense of isolation and mystery without relying on constant combat or cheap jump-scares.

## 3. Gameplay Mechanics (Responsive Inputs)

- **Point-and-Click Exploration:** Players can navigate the station, inspect objects, and interact with environmental elements using intuitive mouse controls.
- **Inventory System:** Collect keycards, tools, access codes, and other useful objects required to unlock restricted areas and solve puzzles.
- **Interactive Objects:** Computers, communication devices, doors, terminals, and damaged machinery feature script-driven interactions.
- **Environmental Puzzles:** Restore power, repair communication systems, decode mysterious transmissions, and uncover hidden information.
- **Dialogue & Logs:** Discover audio recordings, computer messages, and personal logs that reveal the story of the missing crew.

## 4. Progression & Content

- **Progression Loop:** The game features 3 chapters (levels), each introducing new locations, story revelations, and increasingly complex puzzles.
- **Chapter 1 — The Outpost:** Explore the abandoned station, restore basic power, and investigate the mysterious transmission.
- **Chapter 2 — The Signal:** Access restricted research laboratories, decode encrypted messages, and discover what the crew was studying.
- **Chapter 3 — Beyond the Airlock:** Reach the station's communication array, uncover the truth behind the signal, and make a final decision that determines the ending.
- **Content Variety:** The game includes inventory-based puzzles, hidden access codes, terminal interactions, environmental riddles, and narrative discoveries.

As the story progresses, the station's visual atmosphere changes. Flickering lights, malfunctioning displays, distorted transmissions, and unusual environmental effects make the player question what is actually happening.

## 5. Tech Stack & Features

- **Game Engine:** Unity 2D.
- **Programming Language:** C#.
- **Adaptive UI:** Menus, dialogue boxes, and inventory elements adapt to different screen resolutions, supporting both PC and potential mobile builds.
- **Interaction System:** Reusable scripts manage clickable objects, interactive doors, terminals, and puzzle triggers.
- **Inventory Management:** A simple inventory system allows players to collect, select, inspect, and use items.
- **Chapter Management:** A level progression system tracks the current chapter and unlocks new areas when objectives are completed.
- **Persistent Saves:** Game progress, unlocked chapters, inventory state, and audio settings are saved between sessions using Unity's PlayerPrefs system.
- **Audio & Atmosphere:** Ambient space sounds, radio static, warning alarms, and environmental audio create an immersive sci-fi experience.

## 6. Project Goals

The main goal of The Last Signal is to create a compact but immersive 2D adventure that combines sci-fi storytelling, exploration, and logical puzzles.

The project emphasizes modular C# scripts, reusable interaction components, organized Unity scenes, and maintainable game systems. Its design allows new chapters, interactive objects, puzzles, and story events to be added without restructuring the entire project.
